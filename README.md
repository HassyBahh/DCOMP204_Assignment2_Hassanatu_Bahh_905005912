StudentStay -- Student Accommodation Finder

Project Overview
StudentStay is a proposed student accommodation website designed to help university students find suitable places to stay. It brings accommodation information together in one organized platform so students can search listings, compare options, review property details, and contact accommodation providers.
Course: DCOMP 204 -- Web Design
Project: Web Design Research, Concept & UI/UX Design

Problem Statement
Students often find accommodation information scattered across flyers, social media, messaging groups, personal contacts, and word of mouth. Listings may use inconsistent formats or omit important details such as price, location, facilities, photographs, and availability. This makes it difficult to compare properties and can require students to spend extra time visiting different locations.
Proposed Solution
StudentStay provides a centralized place to explore accommodation listings and review the information needed to make an informed choice.

Main Features
Search for available accommodation.
Filter or choose options by preferred location, budget, and accommodation type.
View property photographs and descriptions.
Check location information, facilities, and availability.
Compare accommodation options.
Contact landlords or accommodation providers.
Facilities may include water, electricity, Wi-Fi, security, parking, and laundry services.

Target Users
Primary users
New university students.
Students moving to a new area.
Students looking for affordable accommodation.
Students changing accommodation.
Students comparing available properties.
Secondary users
Landlords and accommodation providers who want to advertise vacant rooms and communicate with interested students.

User Needs
StudentStay is designed around these key needs:
1. Easy accommodation search.
2. Clear price and budget information.
3. Location details, including convenience for campus.
4. Photographs that show the property
 5. Information about available facilities.
6. Current availability.
7. A way to compare options.
8. Simple contact with the accommodation provider.

User-Centred Design
The project uses user research and personas to understand the goals and concerns of students and landlords.
The design aims to make important information clear and the search process straightforward.

Information Architecture
The project includes: - Sitemap: Organizes website pages and content.
- Navigation structure: Helps users access the main sections.
-  - User journey: Describes the main accommodation-search process.

Student User Journey
Open StudentStay and arrive at the Home page.
Select Find Accommodation.
Enter a preferred location.
Choose a budget and accommodation type.
Browse available listings.
Select a property and review its details, photographs, price, and facilities.
Contact the accommodation provider.

Website Frames
The UI/UX prototype contains five main frames: 1. Home -- Website landing page. 2. About -- Information about StudentStay. 3. Services -- Overview of the services offered. 4. Contact -- Contact information or communication options. 5. Accommodation Search (Main Feature) -- The main feature for finding and reviewing accommodation.
Project Goal
The goal is to make accommodation searching more organized and convenient for university students by presenting useful property information in one place and helping students compare their options.
